Solutions Engineer

Kestrel Analytics · London, UK (hybrid, 2 days onsite) · £75,000 – £90,000 · Posted 5 days ago

About the role

Kestrel Analytics builds reporting tooling for mid-market retailers. Solutions Engineers sit between the customer and the product team: scoping integrations, building proofs of concept, and turning what customers actually need into something the product team can ship.

What you would do

  • Run technical discovery with prospective customers and scope integration work
  • Build proofs of concept against customer data
  • Feed recurring customer needs back into the product roadmap

What we are looking for

  • Strong Python and SQL
  • Customer-facing experience: you are comfortable running a room
  • Production ownership of something you built

Requirements added by the job poster

  • Right to work in the United Kingdom (we are not able to sponsor visas for this role)
  • Willing to be onsite in London two days a week
