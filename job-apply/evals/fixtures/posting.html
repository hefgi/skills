Forward Deployed Engineer

Vantage Health · London, UK (hybrid, 2 days onsite) · £95,000 – £120,000 · Posted 3 days ago · 47 applicants

About the role

Vantage Health builds clinical decision support software for NHS trusts and private providers. We are hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer to work directly with our customers: scoping what they actually need, building it, and running it in production alongside them. You will spend real time on site with clinical teams, and the rest of your week writing code.

This is a delivery role, not a pre-sales role. You will own what you ship. Our customers are hospital trusts, so the work carries genuine consequences and genuine constraints around data handling and auditability.

What you will do

  • Work directly with customer teams to turn vague operational problems into shipped software
  • Build and own retrieval and summarisation systems over clinical documentation
  • Set up evaluation harnesses so we can measure quality before anything reaches a clinician
  • Run systems in production and carry the pager for what you build
  • Hand over cleanly: runbooks, training, and documentation the customer can actually use

What we are looking for

  • 5+ years building production software, with meaningful time spent customer-facing
  • Strong Python. You will be writing it every day
  • Experience with retrieval pipelines and LLM evaluation, or a clear track record of picking up unfamiliar systems fast
  • Comfort with ambiguity. Our customers rarely arrive with a specification
  • Experience in healthcare, or another regulated domain where audit and data handling matter
  • Production ownership: you have run what you built, not handed it to another team

Nice to have

  • Kubernetes in production
  • Experience with NHS data standards (FHIR, HL7)
  • Prior work at an early-stage company

Requirements added by the job poster

  • Right to work in the United Kingdom (we are not able to sponsor visas for this role)
  • Willing to be onsite in London two days a week
  • 5+ years of professional software engineering experience

Process

Intro call, a technical conversation about something you have built, a paid half-day working session with the team, and a final conversation with our CTO. We aim to complete within three weeks.
