# Narrative

## Positioning
Engineer who has both scaled an organisation and been the only person writing
the code. Works best where the problem is not yet well defined and the customer
is in the room.

## Career story
Started in analytics tooling, moved into fintech as a founding engineer, spent
three years scaling an engineering org, and has returned to hands-on delivery
building AI-agent systems for clients.

## Strongest proof points
- Scaled engineering from 12 to 35 in two years
- Cut deploy time from 40 minutes to 6
- Built the data platform serving 40TB across 8 sources

## Motivation
Wants to be closer to customers and to the code than an executive role allows,
without giving up responsibility for outcomes.

## Known gaps
No formal healthcare or medical-devices domain experience. Closest adjacency is
regulated fintech, where the compliance and audit constraints are similar.
No production Kubernetes ownership; has worked alongside platform teams that ran it.
