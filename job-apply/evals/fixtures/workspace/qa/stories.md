# Stories

## Scaling a team through rapid growth
tags: leadership, hiring, scaling, conflict
situation: Team of 12 needed to reach 35 in two years while still shipping.
task: Own hiring, structure, and delivery through the growth.
action: Introduced squads, hired four senior engineers first to anchor them, and
  set up a weekly delivery review that surfaced blockers early.
result: Reached 35 engineers, cut deploy time from 40 minutes to 6.
use_for: tell me about scaling, hardest leadership challenge, biggest impact

## Shipping under an unclear spec
tags: ambiguity, customer, delivery
situation: A customer needed a reporting feature but could not say what the
  report should contain.
task: Ship something useful within six weeks.
action: Sat with three of their analysts, watched what they actually exported to
  spreadsheets, and built that instead of the requested feature.
result: Shipped in four weeks; the customer renewed and expanded the contract.
use_for: ambiguity, customer-facing, tell me about a hard problem
