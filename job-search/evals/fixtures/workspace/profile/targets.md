# Targets

## Track: fde
name: Forward Deployed Engineer
positioning: Hands-on engineer who works directly with customers, from scoping
  the problem to shipping it in production.
titles: Forward Deployed Engineer, Solutions Engineer, Applied AI Engineer
seniority: senior, staff
emphasize: customer-facing delivery, shipping speed, production ownership
deemphasize: headcount managed, budget ownership, org design

## Track: leadership
name: Engineering Leader
positioning: Engineering leader who has scaled teams and still ships code.
titles: VP of Engineering, CTO, Head of Engineering
seniority: executive
emphasize: org scaling, hiring, delivery outcomes, technical strategy
deemphasize: low-level implementation detail

## Avoid
- Roles requiring US work authorization or sponsorship
- Junior or mid-level individual contributor roles
